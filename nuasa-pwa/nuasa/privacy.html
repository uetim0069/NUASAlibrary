Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to run the library (your account, the files you upload and your CBT activity). Sign-in with Google gives us your name, email and photo, never your Google password. We do not sell your data, and you can ask us to delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

NUASA Library (the “Service”, at nuasalibrary.vercel.app) is run by the Nigerian Universities Accounting Students Association, University of Uyo chapter (“NUASA UNIUYO”, “we”, “us”). We help accounting students find past questions and lecture notes, practise with timed CBT, and prepare for professional exams.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address, level (100 to 400) and, if you choose to give it, your matric number.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Files you upload: past questions, notes and other materials, with the course and details you add.
  • Study activity: CBT attempts, scores, saved questions and the files you save for offline use.
  • Payments: when you pay for CBT access or a masterclass, the payment is handled by our payment partner (Paystack) or by bank transfer. We receive confirmation of the payment, your email and the amount. We do not receive or store your card number.
  • Project AI: the topic, department and other text you enter, and the drafts that are produced for you.
  • Device data: small items stored on your device, such as your light or dark mode choice, your sign-in session and offline copies of files you saved. Our hosting providers also keep ordinary technical logs (for example IP address and browser type) for security.

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep your session secure.
  • To show you the library, run CBT practice, unlock paid access you paid for and keep your progress.
  • To review uploads before they are published, and to keep the library safe from misuse.
  • To generate Project AI topics and drafts you ask for.
  • To contact you about your account, payments or important changes to the Service.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We use trusted providers to run the Service, and they process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase for the database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Vercel for hosting the website.
  • Google for Sign in with Google, and for the Gemini service that writes Project AI drafts from the text you enter.
  • Paystack for online payments.

Your name may be visible to NUASA executives who review uploads and manage the library. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the Service and its users.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use your Google name, email and photo only to create and show your account, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is active. Approved files stay in the library so other students can keep using them. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your profile and personal details, subject to anything we must keep by law (for example payment records).

6. Your choices and rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can object to certain uses. You can edit your name from the menu inside the app. For anything else, contact us (section 9). You can also remove Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access rules on our database and limited admin access. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your device safe.

8. Children and changes

The Service is for university students and is not meant for children under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, tell us and we will delete it. We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top, and for major changes we will tell you in the app.

9. Contact us

Questions or requests about your data: reach the NUASA UNIUYO executives through the official NUASA UNIUYO channels, or use the contact details on the library website. Please include the email address of your account so we can find it.
